# Rights and third-party provenance

This repository publishes the team's development and research record. Publication alone does not grant a new project-wide license. Original notices apply to their respective components; no blanket ownership claim is made over external code, maps, game data, or referenced papers.

## UNSW Battlecode

The official toolkit and associated helper/map material originate from [UNSW CPMSoc's unswbc project](https://github.com/unswcpmsoc/unswbc). The installed official distribution includes an MIT license, preserved at [docs/publication/licenses/UNSWBC_LICENSE.txt](docs/publication/licenses/UNSWBC_LICENSE.txt). Preserve that notice when using covered material. Engine installations and compiled dependency caches are excluded from this archive.

## External opponents

The local evaluation environment used external opponents including nicked, TX140907's bots, ctmes, devkadaba, Aarav and others. Local provenance notes explicitly identify several as unlicensed or local-only. Their source is not republished here. [EXTERNAL_SOURCES.json](docs/publication/EXTERNAL_SOURCES.json) records retained source references and the excluded snapshot directories, including candidates whose names alone did not reveal their origin.

The project-created opponents, original bot code, evaluation tools and model artifacts are retained. References to unavailable opponents in frozen plans are historical dependencies, not a promise that every trial runs immediately from a fresh clone.

## Replays, datasets and research

Replay archives and derived datasets preserve the retained evidence used in the project. Public competitor/team names and game identifiers are evidence metadata, not claims of authorship of their strategies. Cited papers and websites remain with their authors and publishers; this archive contains analysis and links, not a redistribution of those publications.
