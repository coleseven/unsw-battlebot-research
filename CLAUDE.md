- **One heavy job at a time, at most 12 workers.** On 28 Sep the PC blue-screened twice (0xBE, 0x1E) and GCC segfaulted, each time while two game-running jobs were stacked (12+12 and 12+6 workers on 16 logical CPUs); single jobs had run for hours fine. A bug made it worse: the in-process build signature included a per-process output path, so every worker recompiled every C++ bot at each batch (8 parallel GCC -O2 builds on top of the games); fixed 28 Sep (workers now reuse the cached library in milliseconds, builds are serialised by a lock). `bcsim.guard` enforces it: `bounded.run` and tune's replay scoring take a machine-wide lock (`results/.experiment.lock`, a second job waits) and cap workers at 12 (`BCSIM_MAX_WORKERS`). The underlying cause of the crashes was a RAM overclock: Windows Memory Diagnostic flagged bad regions and a third BSOD came under one 8-worker job; the RAM now runs at 3600 MHz without the overclock. Ad-hoc scripts that play games in parallel must use `guard.slot()` and `guard.cap_workers()` too, never a bare ProcessPoolExecutor.
